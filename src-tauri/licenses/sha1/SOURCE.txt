The local copy dispatches borrowed native blocks to the SHA-1 compressor.
An incomplete block remains in the digest buffer until it is filled or padded.
The ARM64 and x86 SHA instruction paths load checked words into registers.
The app enables the std feature for the standard CPU detector on ARM64 Linux
and Android. Without std on those targets, the library uses the hardware
backend when the compiler target guarantees its instructions.
